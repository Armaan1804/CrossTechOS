Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding construction financial mechanics, policies, and ledger security.

What is CrossTech Construction OS (BuildLedger)?

BuildLedger is a construction financial management platform for tracking project expenses, centralized funds, receipt evidence, overdraft utilization, and financial audit records across construction sites.

Can I use BuildLedger for a single construction site?

Yes. The Starter Pod plan is specified for one active construction site, making it suitable for independent subcontractors and smaller builders.

Can site managers record expenses without internet access?

The platform is designed for offline-first expense recording. Eligible transactions and receipt images can be stored locally and synchronized when connectivity returns.

Are offline transactions immediately added to the central wallet?

Not necessarily. Offline transactions remain pending until they are synchronized and accepted by the central system. The application clearly distinguishes locally queued expenses from confirmed ledger entries.

How does the central wallet work?

The central wallet provides a unified financial record for the organization. Capital additions, expenses, adjustments, and reversals are recorded through controlled transaction workflows.

What happens when the wallet balance reaches zero?

If the organization has an available overdraft allowance, eligible expenses can continue to be recorded within the approved limit. Transactions exceeding that limit are rejected until additional funding or authorization becomes available.

Can I set different spending limits for different plans?

The specification defines plan-level overdraft ceilings (from ₹50,000 up to ₹25,00,000), with custom limits available for enterprise arrangements.

Does BuildLedger require receipt photographs?

The specified field expense workflow requires receipt image evidence for expense submissions. The exact handling of expenses without conventional receipts is defined by the organization's expense policy.

How does BuildLedger prevent duplicate expenses?

The offline synchronization workflow is designed to use duplicate-resistant retries and controlled server-side transaction processing. This helps prevent the same queued expense from being recorded twice.

Can I manage multiple construction projects?

Yes. Depending on the selected plan, BuildLedger supports multiple active sites, centralized expenditure monitoring, and expanded administrative access.

Can I export reports for accountants?

The stated plans support CSV exports, with more advanced Excel and PDF audit reports included in higher tiers.

How long are receipts stored?

The specified retention periods range from 30 days on Starter Pod to 1 year on Pro Builder and multi-year cloud archiving on Enterprise Infra and Business Contractor.

Can BuildLedger integrate with accounting software?

The specification lists integration options for Tally Prime, SAP Business One, Zoho Books, and QuickBooks through the ERP Integration Bridge or enterprise arrangements.

Does BuildLedger support GST reporting?

The platform includes GST-related exports and enterprise tax reporting capabilities. Exact compliance depends on configured workflows and applicable Indian tax requirements.

Is BuildLedger suitable for large infrastructure companies?

The Business Contractor offering is designed for organizations requiring unlimited sites, expanded administrative access, custom overdraft limits, enterprise identity management, dedicated infrastructure, and ERP integrations.

How secure is financial data?

The design includes organization-level database access controls, authenticated sessions, controlled storage uploads, and an append-only immutable financial ledger.
