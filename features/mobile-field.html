MODULE 03 • MOBILE FIELD EXPENSE MANAGEMENT

Built for Real
Construction Sites.

Construction teams work in basements, high-rise buildings, remote infrastructure projects, and outdoor environments where traditional desktop software is inconvenient. BuildLedger provides a mobile-first experience for site managers and engineers to record expenses quickly in the field.

Start Free 14-Day Trial Inspect Mobile Workflow →

Fast Field Expense Recording & Receipt Capture

Capture essential transaction information, compress receipt images on the device, and maintain field accountability without administrative overhead.

CAPABILITY 01

Fast Expense Recording

Capture essential transaction information through a streamlined mobile workflow: enter the expense amount, select the construction site and category, record transaction details, take a photo of the receipt, and review synchronization status instantly.

CAPABILITY 02

Receipt Capture & Compression

Attach supporting receipt evidence directly to an expense instead of relying on scattered messaging-app photos. The workflow compresses receipt images on the device before uploading them, reducing data transferred over limited mobile connections.

CAPABILITY 03

Standardized Expense Categories

Organize common construction expenses using pre-configured categories: Raw materials, Labor and daily wages (bata), Fuel and diesel, Machinery and equipment rentals, Government permits, and Miscellaneous site expenses.

CAPABILITY 04

Field-Friendly Operation & Visibility

Designed around large touch controls, convenient numeric entry, and instant access to the expense feed, camera capture, and site balance. Review recently recorded expenses, receipts, and sync state directly on your mobile device.
