Centralized Financial Control & Ledger Discipline

BuildLedger is designed around controlled financial transactions, validated balance integrity, and immutable audit trails across all active projects.

CAPABILITY 01

Centralized Financial Control

View organizational funds and project-level expenditure in one place. Monitor financial activity across multiple active construction sites, record capital allocations, and track project expenses against the central wallet with real-time balance and overdraft utilization.

CAPABILITY 02

Double-Entry Financial Discipline

BuildLedger is designed around controlled financial transactions and consistent ledger updates. Each transaction is processed through a validated financial workflow to maintain balance integrity and prevent conflicting concurrent updates.

CAPABILITY 03

Immutable Transaction History

Financial records are designed to be append-only. Existing transactions are not ordinarily edited or deleted. The system supports distinct transaction types: Sanctions (capital added), Expenses (costs incurred), Adjustments (reconciliations), and Reversals (linked corrections).

CAPABILITY 04

Transaction-Level Auditability

Each transaction records organization, construction site, responsible user or site manager, transaction type, amount, expense category, resulting wallet balance, receipt evidence, and timestamp—creating a clearer record of who spent what, where, and how it affected available funds.
