The Principles Guiding BuildLedger

Our commitments to contractors, site supervisors, and finance teams.

01

Field Usability Comes First

If software is too complex to use on a busy jobsite, financial tracking breaks down. BuildLedger is designed for the people doing the work—simple, fast, and accessible directly from a mobile phone.

02

Ledger Integrity Is Non-Negotiable

Financial records must be dependable. BuildLedger uses validated ledger transactions, immutable records, and structured adjustments to provide an audit trail that finance teams and accountants can trust.

03

Offline Operation Is Essential

Construction does not stop when cellular coverage drops. Software built for the industry must continue working in basements, remote sites, and areas with limited connectivity.

04

Evidence Must Accompany Expenditure

A number in a register is not enough. Attaching receipt photographs directly to transactions helps verify expenses, simplify reconciliation, and eliminate painful payment disputes.

05

Simple to Adopt, Rigorous to Scale

A trade subcontractor managing one site should be able to get started in minutes. An infrastructure company overseeing twenty sites should have the visibility and controls required to manage large budgets.
