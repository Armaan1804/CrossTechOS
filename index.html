Stop Construction Cash Leakage Before It Becomes a Bigger Problem.

Construction projects involve constant spending on materials, labor, fuel, equipment rentals, transportation, and permits. When these transactions are recorded through paper vouchers, spreadsheets, and messaging apps, financial visibility can quickly deteriorate. BuildLedger replaces fragmented expense tracking with a unified system designed for the realities of construction sites.

01

Untracked Petty Cash

Site supervisors frequently handle small purchases, labor payments, fuel expenses, and urgent material requirements. Without a consistent record of every transaction, expenses can become difficult to verify and reconcile.

02

Lost Receipts and Duplicate Payments

Receipts shared through WhatsApp groups or stored as paper slips can be misplaced, overlooked, or submitted multiple times. Finance teams need a reliable link between each expense and its supporting evidence.

03

Work Stoppages Due to Delayed Approvals

Strict approval procedures can delay urgent site purchases. When diesel, materials, or labor payments cannot be recorded promptly, construction activities may be interrupted.

04

Delayed Financial Reconciliation

Paper-based registers and disconnected spreadsheets make it difficult to understand how much each project is spending, identify discrepancies, and investigate transactions while the supporting information is still available.
